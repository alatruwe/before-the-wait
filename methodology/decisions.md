# Design decisions

Each design decision, with its date and the reason behind it. The rules themselves are in `pre-registration.md`. Decisions made after the public draft opened are also logged in that file's changelog.

| ID | Decision | Date | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|
| F | The question is self-contained: does the model's inside forecast a "wait" earlier than the outside cues do? FrictionMap's marker lexicon is the motivation and the label source, not the object of study. | 2026-10-10 | The claim should match what the test can back. Results are about this model only. |
| D1 | Labels come from the frozen FrictionMap v1 lexicon regex. A hand audit of 100 randomly drawn onsets measures precision only. The audit happens after freeze and before any per-onset probe score is viewed, and never changes training labels. Audit question (2026-10-18): a real hit *overturns* a prior statement; a re-check that confirms it is a false hit. Conditional on a positive band (2026-10-19). | 2026-10-10, question set 2026-10-18 | Gives a precision number for this lexicon on this model. The guard rule stops anyone retraining on the "real" hits after seeing results. |
| D1+ | Exploratory add-on: probe scores before real vs. false hits. Test-split onsets only; at least 20 per group, otherwise logged as not runnable. Scope (2026-10-18): all four bands, reported together with a 95% interval each and no verdict; expected direction stated in advance (higher before real hits). | 2026-10-10, scope set 2026-10-18 | Asks whether the inside anticipates the word, or the course-correction behind it. An exploratory analysis looks broadly and reports everything it looked at; the multiple-looks penalty applies only to claims, and the add-on makes none. |
| D1− | Other markers this model uses go to an exploratory appendix only. | 2026-10-10 | Picking labels after seeing pilot data would be a fork. |
| D2 | GSM8K is the primary dataset, with MATH500 as the fallback if GSM8K can't reach the minimum event count. | 2026-10-10 | The model is distilled for math, and Zhu et al. studied the same model on math. |
| D3 | A gate at distance 1. Four confirmatory bands (2–5, 6–10, 11–20, 21–40 tokens), each tested on its own with a 4-way correction. Exploratory: an exact-distance curve over 1–40 × all layers, and per-trace plots on 10 seeded traces. Distance is counted in tokens. | 2026-10-11 | Bands pool enough examples per test. Testing each band on its own lets an on/off pattern show. The per-trace plots catch signals tied to moments rather than distances. |
| D4a | Null bar δ = 0.05 AUROC lift (provisional, finalized at freeze). Verdicts are positive, null or inconclusive, by where the interval sits relative to 0 and δ. The full interval is always reported, with secondary verdicts at 0.03 and 0.08. Uncertainty comes from resampling whole problems. Minimum events are computed from pilot counts only. | 2026-10-11 | δ is chosen by what difference would matter. The minimum makes a null reachable rather than only "positive or can't tell." |
| D4b | Primary comparison: the probe vs. one combined outside rival (text + visible uncertainty + position), with the same examples and the same tuning effort. Each rival alone is exploratory. A stronger text rival is built only if a band is positive: hidden states of DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-7B reading the same token ids (2026-10-19; was Qwen2.5-Math-1.5B base, named 2026-10-18). | 2026-10-11, rival model named 2026-10-18, changed 2026-10-19 | "Outside" means all outside cues together. Beating each cue separately could hide that they jointly explain the signal. |
| D4c-1 | "No wait coming" examples are the same token kind, with no marker in the next 100 tokens (60 as a count-based fallback). Positions 41–100 tokens before an onset are excluded. | 2026-10-11 | Positions right before a "wait" are mostly sentence ends. Matching leaves whether a wait follows as the only difference. |
| D4c-2 | Split by problem, about 50 / 15 / 35. The layer is chosen per band on validation, and test is used once per band. | 2026-10-11 | Picking the best of 27 layers on test data would inflate the score. |
| D4c-3 | The gate passes if the model's own odds reach AUROC ≥ 0.75 and the probe lands within 0.10 of them, and no more than 0.03 above them (two-sided clause, 2026-10-19: a probe above the model's own ceiling indicates a token-alignment error). A failed gate means fix, log and rerun before anything else. (Margin revised from 0.05 to 0.10 on 2026-10-17 after the pilot gate check measured model odds at 0.962: the clause exists to catch wiring errors, which show as drops of 0.2 or more, and a 0.05 margin would have demanded a 0.91 probe.) | 2026-10-11, revised 2026-10-17 and 2026-10-19 | The model samples its words, so even one token before, the inside knows odds, not outcomes. |
| D5 | Answer correctness is not a hypothesis. Accuracy is reported as context only. | 2026-10-11 | It's a different question, wrong answers are few on GSM8K, and trace length confounds it. |
| D6 | The design is posted publicly. Before freeze, changes are logged with who raised them. Only generation, counting, reading traces and the model's own odds at distance 1 touch data before freeze. | 2026-10-11, revised 2026-10-24 | Feedback can change the design while no analysis has been run. |
| D10 | Looping traces (a 30-token window of generated ids repeated 3+ times verbatim) are excluded. Traces that hit the length cap without looping are kept and flagged. | 2026-10-17 | A verbatim loop produces near-duplicate events that inflate counts and let a probe memorize. Paraphrased circling produces distinct events. Pilot: 5 of 30 loop (4 at the length cap, 1 finished normally); 4 more hit the cap without looping; 9 of 30 affected in all. Looping is not a subset of truncation, so both flags are kept. |
| D11 | Onsets are defined by the paragraph rule: the first hit in a paragraph is the onset, later hits in the same paragraph merge into it. No alternative onset rule is tested in this experiment. | 2026-10-17 | The model delimits its own reasoning steps with blank lines, so the rule is structural and needs no tuned number. Pilot: 192 of 619 within-trace hits share a paragraph with an earlier hit, and nearly half of those are within 3 tokens ("Wait, hold on"). The rule is conservative: it merges some later reconsiderations in the same step, which costs count, not bias. |
| D12 | Trace count: the §4 rule must be met in all four bands, inflated by 20% and rounded up to ten; cap 1,000. Result: 190 GSM8K traces. Recomputed 2026-10-19 under the revised positive rule, m assumptions (AUROC 0.60, ρ 0.30) and 1:2 matching: N = 320. | 2026-10-17, recomputed 2026-10-19 | At the three-band count (143) band 21–40 could never return a null, and it is the band where a null says the most about how far back anticipation reaches; 14 more traces buy it back. The onsets-per-trace estimate rests on 13 pilot traces, so a margin guards against the full run coming in lower. More traces narrow uncertainty in either direction and cannot favor a verdict. |
| D13 | Outside includes the output state; probe reads layers 1–27. The layer-28 (post-final-norm) hidden state joins the rival. | 2026-10-19 | Entropy is one number from the logits and layer 28 is the logits' pre-image; a late-layer win would be output information. |
| D14 | δ = 0.05 and gate floor 0.75 finalized; justification in Appendix A. | 2026-10-24 | Anchors unchanged since the provisional values were set. |
| D15 | Post-freeze data contact is recorded at the top of `results/run_summary.md`, not in the pre-registration's §15. | 2026-11-13 | §15 covers contact before freeze and §16 is for deviations; generating per §4 is neither. The frozen file stays untouched. |
| D16 | The post-generation flag is per band: projected test onsets = 0.35 × eligible onsets in the run, against m = 555. The two older thresholds (onsets per non-looped trace < 7.0; looping share > 25%) are reported as a secondary line. | 2026-11-13 | The older thresholds can stay silent while band 21–40 falls below m, and neither looks at e_b. The thresholds were never in the pre-registration. |
| D17 | No top-up. If a band is short, N stays 320, the shortfall gets a §16 row, and §11's clause applies (that band can only be positive or inconclusive). | 2026-11-13 | §4 fixes N and §14 is silent on a top-up before any probe exists. Decided before the run's counts existed, so the choice can't follow the numbers. |
| D18 | If a token kind's negatives run out in a split, the unmatched positives of that kind are dropped, and the count is reported per distance set and split. | 2026-11-14 | §6 is silent on exhaustion. Dropping keeps kind matching, the no-reuse rule and the 100-token quiet zone intact. Decided before any count from the example-building step existed. |
| D19 | The rival's entropy features (§8) use the raw softmax of the logits: temperature 1, no top-p, in nats. | 2026-11-14 | §8 says "the model's next-token distribution" without naming one. Temperature and top-p are decoding settings; the raw output is the model's own distribution, and the one the pilot gate check used. Decided before any logit of the run was read. |
| D20 | The rival's look-back windows (§8: the 16-token entropy window, the text window, tokens since the last paragraph break) count over every token up to t, prompt included. The entropy window excludes t itself. | 2026-11-14 | §8 does not say where a window stops near the start of reasoning. Its causal-only rule allows any token up to t. One rule, no special case, and the rival gets everything visible. Decided before any probe or rival score existed. |
| D21 | Rival implementation (§8), fixed before any band score existed: the text window includes the token at t; TF-IDF with library defaults and n-grams seen in at least 2 train examples; dense columns z-scored and every feature group scaled to the output-state group's average row length; position counts fall back to the start of the input; same classifier recipe as the probe; chosen rival fitted on train only and saved by hash. | 2026-11-14 | §8 fixes the features and the grid, not these. Each choice was made to avoid handicapping the rival. |
| D22 | Verdict procedure (§10, §11), fixed before the test split was read: percentile interval (0.625th and 99.375th percentiles of the 10,000 paired lifts); bootstrap and shuffled-labels seed 20261019, a fresh generator per band; a resample draws whole test problems and an example belongs to the trace it sits in; the scoring code is rehearsed on validation, then the shuffled-labels control runs, then the test scores; verdict conditions applied to unrounded values. | 2026-11-15 | §10 and §11 fix the method, not these details. Each is the plainest reading of the registered text. |
| D23 | Hand-audit procedure (§12), fixed before the draw. The population is the 806 test-split onsets sorted by (problem index, token index), drawn with `random.Random(20261020).sample(population, 100)` and read in the order the draw returns. Each item shows the problem, the five paragraphs before the marker's paragraph (earlier reasoning collapsed), the marker's paragraph and the two after it, with no score, band, distance, trace id or answer; paragraphs are the pieces between blank lines, empty ones included. Every item is labeled real or false. The change must be visible in the marker's paragraph or the two after it, measured against what the trace held just before the marker; a position it had already dropped does not count. Real only if the change can be pointed to in the shown text, with an `unsure` flag allowed. A marker matched inside another word, or not used as a reconsideration, is false and flagged `mishit`. The interval is a 95% percentile bootstrap over whole problems, 10,000 resamples, seed 20261020. The draw, sheet and scoring script are committed before labeling; the labels and their sha256 in one commit after all 100, frozen from then on. The FrictionMap side of the side-by-side quotes only figures public in frictionmap's `project_design/HOW_IT_WORKS.md`. | 2026-11-15, finalized 2026-11-19 | §12 fixes the sample size, seed, question and timing, not these details. §12 says "after the marker" without a limit; the two-paragraph window and the forced label make the question answerable from a fixed text, and both can only lower precision. The window was tried on five train-split onsets before the draw: the rest of the reasoning changed none of the five calls. Resampling whole problems is the rule used for every other interval in the experiment. Decided before any audit item was drawn or read. |
| D24 | Stronger text rival for band 6–10 (§8), fixed before any 7B state existed. The reader is the 7B alone, one logistic regression per layer on its hidden state at t, with the probe's recipe, examples and labels; every 7B layer is eligible, the final one included, chosen with C on validation AUROC. Model revision `916b56a4`, fp16, the stored token ids never re-tokenized; token-id compatibility rechecked on all 292 kept traces before any weight is loaded. The chosen reader is fitted on train, saved and hashed before test. The verdict uses D22's procedure and §11's table unchanged (98.75% interval, δ = 0.05, secondary 0.03 and 0.08), on the lift of the frozen 1.5B probe over the 7B reader; claim level 2 holds only on a positive verdict. This scores the band 6–10 test examples a second time, as §8 registers; the frozen probe is not refitted and its test AUROC must reproduce exactly. | 2026-11-21 | §8 names the model, the recipe and the two claim levels, not these details. Giving the 7B its final layer and the full verdict rule avoids handicapping the rival and keeps one definition of "beats". Decided before the 7B weights were downloaded. |
| D25 | Exploratory analyses (§13), fixed before any of them ran. Analyses 1, 2, 3 and 7 are reported on the test split, with nothing chosen or tuned on it. The exact-distance curve uses the per-layer band probes at their validation-best C, sliced by exact distance, each negative counted at its positive's distance, with the band's frozen rival for the lift; distance 1 uses the gate probes and has no lift; the band seams are marked. Each rival group alone is fitted with D21's recipe and chosen on validation; the text group is re-tuned alone over the rival's full registered grid, so that it is not handicapped. Per-trace plots use 10 test problems drawn with `random.Random(20261021)` and the four frozen band probes at every reasoning token. The paragraph analysis and accuracy use labels and text only. The same-trace rerun scores the frozen probe and rival on test with cross-trace negatives removed, no refit. The add-on is not runnable (13 real hits, 20 needed). No verdict is applied to any of these. | 2026-11-21 | §13 lists the analyses, not how each is computed. Each choice is the smallest computation that answers the listed question, and none changes a confirmatory number. Decided after the confirmatory verdicts, the audit and the stronger rival were known, which is the registered order (§13: "done after the confirmatory results"). |

## Parked, with revival conditions

| Item | Revival condition |
|---|---|
| Onsets labeled by an LLM judge instead of the lexicon | A positive band |
| Distance counted in reasoning steps instead of tokens | A positive band at 11–20 or 21–40 |
| Incremental comparison (the probe added on top of the rival) | A band where the probe is well above chance but doesn't beat the rival |
| Replication on DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-7B: the whole experiment on the 7B, its own internals forecasting its own "wait" (not the D4b stronger rival, where the 7B's hidden states read the 1.5B's text to forecast the 1.5B's "wait"; that the 7B writes "wait" itself makes it a competent reader that represents reconsideration) | A positive result on the 1.5B model |
| Probe score as a predictor of answer correctness | A positive band and at least 50 wrong answers in the test set |
| Window-rule onsets (merge hits within 10 tokens) as an alternative to the paragraph rule | A positive band, and a reader or the writeup needs to know whether the verdict depends on the onset rule |
| Random-direction control (does a random direction through the activations separate the classes, which would mean they differ in activation size rather than in a feature) | A positive band, and a reviewer asks whether matching by token kind left a size difference |
